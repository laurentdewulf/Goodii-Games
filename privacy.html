Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

GOODII Games ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you play Cap Tap, our mobile game.

1. Information We May Collect

Cap Tap does not currently require you to create an account or provide your name, mailing address, or phone number to play. However, the game and services integrated into it may process certain information.

  • Device and advertising information: device identifiers, advertising identifiers where available, IP address, device type, operating system, language, approximate location derived from IP address, and advertising preferences or consent choices.
  • Advertising activity: information about ads displayed, viewed, clicked, or completed, and related information used for ad delivery, measurement, frequency controls, and fraud prevention.
  • Purchase information: transaction and purchase-status information needed to process in-app purchases, restore eligible purchases, and provide purchased content or remove ads. Payment card details are handled by the relevant app store, not collected directly by GOODII Games through Cap Tap.
  • Locally stored game information: the game may store progress, scores, selected caps, points, purchase entitlements, and settings on your device.
  • Technical information: information that may be available to our advertising or platform providers for security, service operation, and troubleshooting.

The exact information processed depends on your device, operating system, consent choices, and the services enabled in the version of Cap Tap you use.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Cap Tap uses Unity LevelPlay advertising mediation with the ironSource advertising network enabled in our current configuration. We may display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.

These services may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, and other information described in their own privacy documentation. They may use information for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and other purposes described in their policies.

We do not control the independent privacy practices of third-party service providers. Please review their documentation:

  • Unity Privacy Policy
  • Unity LevelPlay Legal Resources

Advertising network integrations may change as the game develops. We will update this policy when our data practices materially change.

3. In-App Purchases

Cap Tap may offer optional purchases, including points packs and a purchase to remove advertisements. Purchases are handled through the applicable app store's purchasing system.

We may receive purchase confirmations, product identifiers, transaction references, or entitlement status needed to deliver purchased items, restore eligible purchases, and prevent fraud. The applicable store's privacy policy also governs its handling of your purchase information.

4. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain Cap Tap.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Process purchases and restore eligible entitlements.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized transactions.
  • Maintain security and comply with legal obligations.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your email address to third parties. Information may be processed or shared as necessary with advertising providers, app stores, technical service providers, and authorities where required by law.

Third-party providers may handle information under their own policies and contractual arrangements. Their processing may include advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and service delivery.

6. Data Retention and Local Storage

Some game settings and progress may remain stored locally on your device until they are removed by the game, overwritten, or cleared when you delete the app or its data. Deleting the game may not delete information already held by an app store or third-party provider.

Third-party providers determine their own retention periods under their policies and applicable obligations. We retain information we receive only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law.

7. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or information about certain personal data, or to opt out of certain processing. You may contact us using the address below to make a request.

You can also manage available device-level privacy and advertising settings through your operating system. Some requests may need to be directed to the relevant third-party provider or app store.

Where required, we will provide appropriate choices or obtain consent before relevant data processing. The availability of specific choices depends on your location, device, and the services used by the game.

8. Children's Privacy

Cap Tap is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children under 13.

Because advertising services may process device or advertising information, the game's audience, age rating, advertising configuration, and applicable consent requirements must be considered together. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us so we can review the request.

9. International Users

Depending on your location and the services used, information may be processed in countries other than your own. Applicable privacy protections and rights may vary by jurisdiction.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information within our control. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Cap Tap, our service providers, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date where appropriate.
