Purchase and No Ads help

If a purchase doesn't appear in your game, or advertisements continue after buying the No Ads option, please contact us.

  • Tell us which item or purchase is affected.
  • Include the device model and operating system version.
  • Describe what happened and any error message you saw.
  • For purchase problems, include the store order or transaction reference if available. Never send your password or full payment card details.

Eligible purchases may be restorable through the game's Restore Purchases option, where available.

Report a game issue

If Cap Tap freezes, closes unexpectedly, or behaves incorrectly, please tell us what you were doing when the problem occurred. Steps to reproduce the issue are especially helpful.
